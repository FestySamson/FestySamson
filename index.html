Physics taught me to break problems down. Websites let me build the answers.

I'm a Nigerian digital professional working across web development, UI/UX design, digital marketing, graphics and business operations. I studied Physics at the Federal University of Petroleum Resources, Effurun (FUPRE) and graduated in May 2023.

That training shaped how I work: understand the problem first, plan the structure, then design and build. I've applied it to school, real estate, fintech, agriculture and digital-service websites, always asking what the visitor needs to understand and do next.

I've also supervised restaurant teams, managed community and marketing work for a crypto exchange project, and taught Mathematics and Physics during NYSC. Those roles taught me to communicate clearly, stay accountable and keep a business running.

My approach: technology should make something easier for a real person, whether that's a parent finding school news or a Nigerian abroad exploring property back home.
